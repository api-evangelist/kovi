# Kovi

> Kovi (Kovi Tecnologia S.A.) is a Brazilian mobility company founded in 2018 that rents and subscribes cars to ride-hailing and delivery app drivers across Latin America. It offers monthly, annual and pay-per-kilometer plans bundling maintenance, protection and vehicle documentation, plus a purchase option on its Kovi Proprio product. Kovi operates in twelve Brazilian cities and in Mexico, and employs more than 1,400 people.

## Important note for agents

Kovi publishes **no public API, no developer portal and no API documentation**. Its production API at `https://api.kovi.us/graphql` is private and authentication-gated: unauthenticated requests return HTTP 401 and GraphQL introspection is unavailable. Do not attempt programmatic integration — there is no public credential path. Driver and customer interaction happens through the web and mobile apps below.

## Products

- [Monthly rental](https://www.kovi.com.br/alugar-carro-mensal): Month-to-month rental of certified pre-owned vehicles with auto-renewal
- [Kovi Proprio](https://www.kovi.com.br/comprar-carro-proprio): Annual plan on a 0km vehicle with a purchase option from month 13
- [Pay per kilometer](https://www.kovi.com.br/pague-o-km-que-rodar): Usage-based rental pricing
- [Contracted maintenance](https://www.kovi.com.br/manutencao-contratada): Included maintenance program
- [Escola Kovi](https://www.kovi.com.br/escola-kovi): Driver education program
- [Direcao Segura](https://www.kovi.com.br/direcao-segura): Safe-driving program
